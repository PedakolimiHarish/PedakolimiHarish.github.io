ROS2 Developer — Construction Robotics & 3D Printing

    Project Overview

    At Kelvin6k Technologies, I worked on SCARA-based construction 3D-printing systems, developing ROS2 software and supporting the integration of robotic, mechanical, and electronic systems for real-world construction applications.

    A major part of the work involved automating the G-code preparation workflow using Python, reducing preparation time by approximately 20 minutes per print job. I also developed a robot alignment workflow that reduced setup time from days to minutes, improving the practicality of deploying the robotic printing systems.

    The work extended beyond software development into deployment, operation, and maintenance, supporting three construction-printing projects and collaborating with software, electronics, and mechanical teams to deliver field-ready robotic systems.
