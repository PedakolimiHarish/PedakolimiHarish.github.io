Project Ember

Open Source AI Companion Robot Platform

Project Ember is an open-source robotics platform focused on building a complex, long-term physical AI system. Its initial development will focus on basic autonomous navigation, embedded systems, computer vision, and ROS 2, while following industry-standard components, engineering practices, and robotics methodologies wherever practical.

The long-term goal is to move beyond conventional autonomous robots toward physical AI—a robot capable of interacting naturally with people and its environment, understanding context, remembering individuals and previous interactions, and developing a consistent personality and behavior over time.

Because of the breadth of the system, Project Ember is intentionally being developed as a long-term project. The platform will be built incrementally, establishing reliable hardware, control, perception, navigation, and software foundations before progressively introducing more advanced intelligence and interaction capabilities.
