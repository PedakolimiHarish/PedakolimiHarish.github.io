Four-Bar Workspace Envelope Simulator

Interactive Four-Bar Mechanism Kinematics & Workspace Analysis

Four-Bar Workspace Envelope is a completed university project that developed an interactive tool for analyzing the kinematics and workspace of planar four-bar mechanisms.

    Project Overview

    The project focuses on the computational analysis and visualization of a planar four-bar linkage. It allows users to define the mechanism geometry and analyze its motion, coupler trajectory, workspace envelope, velocity, and acceleration through an interactive interface.

    The project combines mechanical engineering theory with numerical computation and web-based visualization to provide an interactive way to study four-bar mechanism behavior and workspace characteristics.
