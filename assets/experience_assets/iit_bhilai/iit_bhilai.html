IIT Bhilai

M.Tech. in Applied Mechatronics & Robotics

At IIT Bhilai, I pursued a Master's degree in Applied Mechatronics and Robotics, developing a broader understanding of robotic systems across mechanical design, embedded systems, control, simulation, perception, and software.

The program provided the foundation for several of my robotics projects, including work involving ROS 2, robot simulation, autonomous mobile robots, robotic manipulation, computer vision, and motion planning. It also gave me the opportunity to work across disciplines rather than treating robotics as purely a software or mechanical problem.

During this period, I also developed projects that connected academic concepts with practical robotics engineering, including the Four-Bar Workspace Envelope, Mobile 3D Printer, and my work toward robotic construction systems.
