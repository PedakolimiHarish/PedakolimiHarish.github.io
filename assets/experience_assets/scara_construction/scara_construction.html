SCARA Construction 3D Printer

SCARA Robotic System for Construction-Scale 3D Printing

A SCARA-based robotic system developed for construction-scale 3D printing, focusing on robot control, motion execution, and hardware integration for real-world printing operations.

    Project Overview

    The project involved the design and development of a SCARA-based FDM clay 3D printer for construction-scale additive manufacturing. The complete system was developed from the mechanical concept through to a functional physical prototype, including the robotic structure, printing hardware, and supporting mechanical components.

    The SCARA mechanism's kinematics and motion execution were handled by Duet 3D firmware, allowing the robot to interpret printing commands and execute the required coordinated motion. My work covered the complete mechanical development process, including CAD design, component development, assembly, integration, and conversion of the digital design into a physical working system.

    The project provided practical experience across robotic mechanism design, CAD-to-reality development, motion control, 3D-printing systems, and hardware integration. It was particularly valuable as an end-to-end engineering project because the system was not limited to simulation or CAD—the mechanical design was actually manufactured, assembled, integrated, and tested as a physical construction-printing platform.
