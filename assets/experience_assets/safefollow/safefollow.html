SafeFollow

Autonomous Companion & Home Security Robot

SafeFollow was a competition project focused on developing an autonomous mobile companion and home-security robot combining embedded control, computer vision and sensor integration. Development was discontinued after the main controller board was damaged during testing.

    Project Overview

    SafeFollow was developed for the Arduino Physical AI Challenge India 2026 as an exploration of a physical AI companion and home-security robot. The project was built around an Arduino UNO Q and used a differential-drive system with wheel encoders, an IMU, a 720p camera, a speaker and amplifier, two 4×4 LED matrices for expressive eyes, and a pan-tilt mechanism for head movement.

    A dedicated web-based dashboard was developed to operate and monitor the robot, supporting both fully manual and autonomous control. The dashboard also provided system monitoring and logging, allowing robot events and system behavior to be observed during development and testing.

    The project brought together mobile robot control, sensing, computer vision, audio feedback, physical expression, and software integration into a single platform. Development was ultimately halted after the Arduino UNO Q was damaged during testing, preventing the remaining hardware and software integration from being completed.

    The ideas and experience gained from SafeFollow later became an important inspiration for Project Ember, which expands the concept into a much larger, long-term Physical AI robotics platform focused on robust engineering, natural interaction, memory, personality, and autonomous behavior.
